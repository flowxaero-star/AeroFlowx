Who we are

A club that
waits for you

We are a free community running club based in Moodbidri. Whether you're on your first kilometre or your first marathon block, everyone is welcome. Our goal is to make fitness enjoyable through consistency, community, and shared progress.

No membership. No qualifying pace. Nobody gets left behind on a route — the group turns back for the last runner, every single time.

Questions

Before you
turn up

Anyone aged 14 and above. Runners under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign the form and come along for the first session. There's no fitness test and no qualifying pace.

Yes. No membership fee, no event fee, no equipment to buy. The club is run by volunteers. The only thing we ever collect is optional donations on charity run Sundays, and that goes straight to the cause.

Most of our members started with a run-walk. Every Sunday has a beginner pace group led by a volunteer who stays at the back. If you need to walk, the group walks with you.

Jain Temple Road, Moodbidri, for most sessions. Trail and festival runs start elsewhere — the exact point goes out on WhatsApp the evening before, with a map pin.

Running shoes, a water bottle, and comfortable clothes. That's it. For trail runs bring shoes with grip. For anything over 10 km, eat something light an hour before.

Nothing happens. There's no attendance record and no streak to lose. Come back on any Sunday and start from wherever your legs are.
